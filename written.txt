2b.
There is a tracked drawingorder in which particle spawns in first to the other. We can take advantnage of this 
and use the built in OpenGL GL_DEPTH_BUFFER_BIT and glEnable(GL_DEPTH_TEST) to give a depth amount depending on 
when that particle was drawn and it's distance to another particle drawn before or after. By doing this to every pixel, 
we can give depth to our particles.
